📢 Important Announcement: YouTube Watch Tasks Update

Hello everyone,

To improve task quality and ensure proper completion, a new requirement will be applied to all future YouTube watch tasks.

✅ Requirement

Before starting any YouTube watch task (including search-based viewing or direct playback), you must enable the “Stats for Nerds” setting as shown in the tutorial video.

How to enable:
1. Open the video on YouTube
2. Right-click on the video player
3. Select “Stats for nerds”

Once enabled, task completion will be based on the Dropped Frames count shown in Stats for nerds — not a fixed minimum watch time (e.g. 4500 / 7500, or as assigned per task).

ℹ️ Why this changed
This helps us verify that the video was actually played with the required setting enabled, keeping tasks fair for everyone.

📌 Important Notes

• Enable Stats for nerds BEFORE you start watching
• Follow all task instructions carefully — do not skip steps
• Check your task page for the exact Dropped Frames target
• Submit proof only after the required count is reached
• Submitting screenshots without proper completion may result in rejection; repeated violations may affect your account

🎥 Tutorial

Please watch the attached tutorial video carefully and follow the steps exactly before starting any YouTube watch task.

⏰ Effective immediately for all new YouTube watch tasks (direct and search-based).

Thank you for your cooperation. 🦊
