📢 Important Notice – FoxiGrow Project Activities & Task System

Dear Members,

Our project is based on two main types of activities and two types of tasks.

✅ 1. Regular Bot Activity

- Stay active in the bot.
- Complete tasks regularly.
- Earn as a normal user by participating in available opportunities.

✅ 2. Community Activity

- Stay active in our official Telegram group.
- Help new and existing users.
- Share useful information and assist in solving problems.
- Become eligible for the Task Master role by making meaningful contributions to the community.

📌 Two Types of Tasks

🌍 Global Tasks

These tasks are provided directly through the bot and are available to all eligible users.

🔒 High-Value Private Tasks

These tasks are reserved for users who are active in the community. Participants are selected from the Telegram group and invited to complete these tasks privately.

If you want access to these exclusive private events in addition to the regular bot tasks, you must stay active in the group and contribute by helping other users.

⚠️ Important: Simply sending unnecessary messages or chatting to increase your activity count has no value. Real community activity means helping others, sharing useful information, discussing new ideas, and contributing to problem-solving.

Thank you for being a valuable part of the FoxiGrow community.
