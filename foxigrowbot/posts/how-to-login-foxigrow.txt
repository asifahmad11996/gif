🔐 How to Log In to Your FoxiGrow Account

You can access your FoxiGrow account on the website using Telegram or Google.

1️⃣ Log In with Telegram
Open Google Chrome or your preferred browser.
Visit foxigrow.com.
Click Start Earning.
Complete the CAPTCHA verification.
Select Continue with Telegram.
Complete the authorization in Telegram.
Once authorized, you’ll be logged in and can access available tasks.

2️⃣ Log In with Google

If you’ve already connected a Google account to your FoxiGrow account:

Open foxigrow.com.
Click Continue with Google.
Select your connected Google account.
Complete the login process.

⚠️ Important: Link Your Google Account

We strongly recommend connecting a Google account to your FoxiGrow account for account recovery and additional security.

After logging in with Telegram:

👉 Profile → Link → Google Account → Connect

You can also connect your Google account through your FoxiGrow Telegram account using the same option.

💡 Why connect Google?

If you ever lose access to your Telegram account, a connected Google account can help you recover access to your FoxiGrow account and associated data.

🔐 Connect your Google account today and keep your FoxiGrow account protected.
